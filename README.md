# CLEP Mathematics Prep
## Lessons, practice and structured review

**Project dates: September 2026 – September 2026**

CLEP Mathematics Prep helps students organize preparation for the CLEP College Mathematics exam through lessons, practice, mastery tracking and spaced review. Students are using the application for exam preparation.

**Context:** Individually developed education application. Student use is confirmed; exam pass rates and learning gains have not been measured.

## Who it is for and what it solves

Learners need more than a collection of questions: they need a way to study topics, see where more practice is needed and revisit material over time. The application connects those activities into a structured study workflow.

## How it works

1. Work through released lessons and the structured study plan.
2. Complete practice activities and receive feedback.
3. Mastery rules consider recent attempts, difficulty and hints.
4. Review scheduling brings due topics and mistakes back into practice.
5. Progress is retained in the learner's browser for continued study.

The implementation includes browser-local IndexedDB progress storage, deterministic mastery and review logic, Vitest tests and Playwright responsive checks. A dated September 2026 acceptance report records successful automated checks.
